KALKATTA OPTICAL

Privacy Policy · Last updated: 3 November 2026

Kalkatta Optical ("we", "our", "us") is an eyewear retail shop. This policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. It applies to our shop records, our website, and the WhatsApp messages we send you.

1. Information we collect

  • Contact details — your name, phone/WhatsApp number and (optionally) email address.
  • Prescription details — SPH, CYL, AXIS and ADD values you provide for your eyewear order, and any prescription image you upload.
  • Order and invoice records — items purchased, amounts, invoices and visit history needed for billing and after-sales service.
  • Messages you send us — the content of your WhatsApp enquiries and orders.

2. How we use your information

  • To fulfil your eyewear order and provide fitting, delivery and after-sales service.
  • To send your invoice, order updates and — only if you have opted in — order-related messages on WhatsApp.
  • To maintain accurate billing and accounting records required by law.
  • To answer your enquiries and support requests.

3. WhatsApp messaging

We send invoices and order updates through the official WhatsApp Business platform. Messages are delivered with the help of Meta's WhatsApp service, which processes your phone number and message content in accordance with Meta's Privacy Policy.

  • We only message numbers that have given us consent (opt-in).
  • Promotional or bulk messages are sent only with your separate, explicit opt-in, and you can opt out at any time by replying STOP or by contacting us.
  • We never use your number for any purpose other than the messages described here.

4. Prescription uploads

Prescription images you upload are stored securely on our systems, are accessible only to our staff for order processing, and are never exposed through public links.

5. Sharing and sale of data

  • We do not sell your personal data to anyone.
  • We share data only with the service that delivers our WhatsApp messages (Meta/WhatsApp) and where required by law.

6. Data retention and security

We keep order and invoice records for as long as needed for billing, warranty and legal requirements. Shop systems are protected by access controls and encrypted backups. We retain data only as long as necessary and delete or anonymise it when it is no longer required.

7. Your rights

  • Ask us what personal data we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate data.
  • Ask us to delete your data, where we are not legally required to keep it.
  • Withdraw your WhatsApp opt-in at any time.

8. Children

Our services are directed at customers aged 18 or above. For minors, the order must be placed by a parent or guardian.

9. Cookies

Our website uses only the strictly necessary cookies required to keep you signed in and to operate the site. We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will always show when it was most recently changed.
